The house

Not a hotel built to look like a home — a home that happens to have rooms to spare.

Highway Homestay sits just off Adampur Road in Bhogpur, close enough to the highway for an easy stop, quiet enough once you're through the gate to forget it's there.

The rooms are family bedrooms, not units — old wooden furniture, a wardrobe with a mirror, curtains that have seen a few winters. Out back, a courtyard opens onto an orchard of mango trees, a shallow plunge bath for the summer heat, and a wood-fired pizza oven the family built themselves.

Come for a night on the highway, or take the whole house for a family gathering — either way, someone will hand you a key personally.
